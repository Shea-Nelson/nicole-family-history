Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Published at https://shea-nelson.github.io/nicole-family-history/

Every statement is labeled Confirmed, Likely, or Unverified. Living people are named by first name only. Photographs are openly licensed or public-domain pictures of places, not family portraits.
